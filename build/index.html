01 · Main build

Airsup

Software for teams building complex physical products. The current focus is making manufacturing information and production decisions usable by both people and AI.

2026 — now
Founder & CEO
Airsup

One live record for the physical product

Airsup connects the files and decisions around a build — CAD, BOMs, PDFs, quotes, suppliers and production changes — so a hardware team can work from the current state instead of reconstructing it across tools.

Customer discovery100+ hardware teams and factories studied across aerospace, robotics and manufacturing.
Current productAI-assisted product records plus manufacturing and factory simulation workflows.
Launch motionPersonally onboarding the first cohort of teams rather than handing early deployment to a sales layer.

Factory simulation experiments

Factories as executable systems

A series of interactive factory experiments used to explore production flow, bottlenecks, machine placement and AI-assisted decision making before changes are made on a real floor.

Jet factory

Aircraft manufacturing flow translated into an interactive 3D plant model and production system.

Robotaxi factory

Production-line simulation around a high-complexity physical product with repeatable manufacturing steps.

Humanoid factory

Manufacturing exploration for robotics assembly, sequencing and production constraints.

Launch systems

Experiments extending the same approach to aerospace and launch infrastructure.
