01 / Current build

Airsup

A live record for complex hardware: the product, its parts, checks, changes, suppliers and decisions in one place.

Founder & CEO · 2026–now

Make the physical state visible.

I founded Airsup after talking with hardware teams that were reconstructing production truth from CAD, spreadsheets, PDFs and messages.

BuiltLed the first product: product records, manufacturing workflows and AI assisted operations.
Hard partKeeping revisions and decisions connected without adding another manual system.
LearnedUseful factory AI needs current state and a traceable reason for each change.
Result100+ team and factory conversations informed the product; I onboarded the first paid enterprise users.

02 / Applied experiments

Factory simulations

Interactive models for testing production flow, station placement and bottlenecks before a real line changes.

Jet / robotaxi / humanoid / launch

Factories as executable systems.

I built 3D factory experiments where the layout and process can change, instead of treating a plant as a static rendering.

Hard partA convincing animation can still model an unrealistic process.
LearnedExplicit stations, routing and constraints matter more than visual polish alone.
