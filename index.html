History

I was born in Dresden, Germany. My parents gave me a stable foundation early on. I spent my childhood playing instruments, building with Lego, and teaching myself to code and solder hardware by 13.

At 15 I started releasing music as Pytro. Within six months I played 30 club shows across Germany and put out remixes that reached up to a million streams on SoundCloud. At 16 I released "Somebody to Love" with MATTN and Klaas. It hit 100,000 streams on Spotify in a single day. The next night I played Bootshaus Cologne, ranked the seventh best club in the world.

In 2023 I started posting remixes on TikTok. "Blonde Chaya" with Niklas Dee went viral and earned Gold status with over 100 million streams through Universal Music. While still in high school I played shows across Europe, worked with influencers and DJs, and ran my own events.

Alongside music I co-founded HUGE Productions GmbH with Tade Mehl. We built the HUGE Conversations podcast and interviewed founders and CEOs across Dubai, London, and Paris.

I moved to San Francisco and spent time with people in engineering and science. I spent the last 6 months on factory floors in China, the US, and the EU, built relationships with 100 manufacturers in person, and learned enough Mandarin to earn direct supplier trust. I read constantly.

In 2026 I started my second company, Airsup. It connects hardware founders with verified manufacturers worldwide through Supi, an AI agent that runs sourcing and negotiation.
