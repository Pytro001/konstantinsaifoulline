About Pytro

At 15, Pytro started releasing music and quickly turned early momentum into live shows. Within six months he played 30 club shows across Germany and released remixes that reached up to a million streams on SoundCloud.

At 16 he released "Somebody to Love" with MATTN and Klaas. The record reached 100,000 Spotify streams in a single day, and the next night he played Bootshaus Cologne, ranked among the best clubs in the world.

In 2023 he began posting remixes on TikTok. "Blonde Chaya" with Niklas Dee went viral, earned Gold status through Universal Music, and passed 100 million streams.

Since 2024 he plays shows across Europe and the United States bringing fast house and techno beats to the clubs and collaborating with Influencers on shows.
